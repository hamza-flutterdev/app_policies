Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy applies to mobile apps published by CodenameHasan. Features and data practices differ between apps; an app handles only the information and services needed for the features it includes. The categories below may not apply to every app.

Information

Depending on the app and features you use, an app may handle information you provide, account details, app content, purchase status, feedback, diagnostics, or AI prompts. Some information may stay on your device; information needed for an online feature may be sent to the app developer or the service providing that feature.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide app features, save your content, manage purchases where available, respond to support requests, and maintain or improve app reliability where diagnostic services are used.

Permissions and Third-Party Services

An app may request device permissions needed for its features. It may also use services such as app-store billing, analytics, sign-in, email, or AI providers. The services used depend on the specific app. Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms. The app listing and Google Play Data safety section provide information about the features and data practices applicable to each app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Retention depends on the app and the feature. Information stored on your device may remain until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Exported files and device backups may need to be deleted separately. Apps that allow account creation provide in-app and external methods to request account deletion.

Children's Privacy

Each app's intended audience is described in its store listing. If a parent or guardian has a privacy concern about information handled by one of our apps, they can contact us below.

Security and Changes

We use the protections available through each app and its platform services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
