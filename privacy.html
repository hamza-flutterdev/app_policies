Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications handle information when you use them. It is written as a general policy that can be used across apps published by the same developer.

Information We Collect

Depending on the app and the features you use, an app may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information from sign-in providers, such as name, email address, user ID, profile photo, and email verification status.
  • App usage, diagnostics, analytics, and crash information through Firebase or similar services.
  • Premium access status, subscriptions, purchases, and related purchase state.
  • Feedback or support information you choose to send, including your name, selected issue type, description, and email content.
  • App activity data needed for features such as saved items, favorites, progress, history, quizzes, or other in-app tools.
  • AI prompt or request content when you choose to use AI-assisted features.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Sign you in and maintain your account where sign-in is available.
  • Provide app content, saved items, progress, history, premium features, and other in-app functionality.
  • Improve app features, reliability, and user experience.
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and performance issues.
  • Respond to feedback or support requests.
  • Verify and maintain premium access, subscriptions, or purchases where applicable.

App Permissions

Some apps may request device permissions so specific features can work properly. These permissions may include, depending on the app:

  • Internet or network access to load content, sync data, process payments, show ads, send feedback, or use online services.
  • Notification permission to send reminders, alerts, or app updates.
  • Camera, microphone, storage, media, location, or other device permissions only when a feature in that app requires them.

Permissions are used only for the feature that needs them. If an app does not include a feature that needs a specific permission, that permission is not requested. You can allow, deny, or change app permissions from your device settings.

Third-Party Services

An app may use third-party services that process data according to their own privacy policies, including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Billing or Apple in-app purchase services
  • AI service providers for AI-assisted responses
  • The user's email app or email provider for feedback and support messages

Please review the privacy policies of these services for more information about their data practices.

AI Requests

When you use AI-assisted features, the app may send your prompt or related request content to the configured AI service so a response can be generated. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into AI prompts or requests.

Feedback

When you submit feedback, an app may open your email application with feedback details filled in or may submit support information through another service. Sending feedback is optional.

Account Deletion

If account deletion is available in an app, deleting your account removes or requests removal of account data controlled by the app. Some records may remain where required for legal, security, billing, backup, or platform compliance purposes.

Children's Privacy

The apps are intended for general, educational, productivity, or utility use depending on the specific app. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information that should be removed, they can contact us.

Data Security

We use Firebase and platform services to help protect app data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
